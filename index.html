Project 3 — AI Model Comparison

Adapted from Coursebook p.46, Ex. 4.5 · 25 pts
Copy the fixed prompt below — the same one for everyone in the class — and paste it, exactly as-is, into each of the three AI tools. Using the same input for every model is what makes comparing their performance meaningful. This order email is our own, written for this exercise — it's inspired by Exercise 4.5's "order confirmation" format but isn't the textbook's email, and it's deliberately a bit more complex (conditional discount, split shipping, a payment deadline to calculate) so the three models have something real to differ on. The prompt also doesn't specify a table format on purpose — how each model chooses to structure it is worth comparing too.
Fixed prompt — paste this exactly into ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini
Subject: Order Confirmation — PO #48213 Dear Ms. Herrera, Thank you for your order. We're writing to confirm PO #48213, placed on 14 August 2026, for the following items: - 250 units of the EcoFlow Reusable Bottle (SKU EF-220) at $6.80 each - 120 units of the EcoFlow Travel Mug (SKU EF-330) at $9.50 each - 400 units of the EcoFlow Lunch Box (SKU EF-410) at $8.25 each As agreed on our call last Tuesday, orders over $3,000 qualify for a 6% discount on the affected line items. Since your Lunch Box order clears that threshold on its own, that line is discounted accordingly; the other two lines are not. The Lunch Boxes are in stock and will ship from our Chonburi warehouse on 22 August, arriving at your Rayong distribution centre within 3–5 business days. The Reusable Bottles and Travel Mugs are currently back-ordered and will ship together from our Bangkok facility on 5 September, arriving at your Bangkok head office instead, per your instructions on the call. Payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date, which will be issued on the date of the first shipment. A 2% early-payment discount applies if the full balance is settled within 10 days of the invoice date. We've enclosed our updated product catalogue for your reference. Please let us know if you have any questions. Sincerely, James Okafor Sales Coordinator, EcoFlow Supply Co. --- Extract the order details from the email above into a table.
